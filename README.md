## ❓ Problem Statement  
In many companies, sales teams collect a lot of lead data but don’t always know where they are losing potential customers. Without clear insights into the sales pipeline, it becomes difficult to improve conversion rates or focus on the right lead sources.

---

## 📌 Overview  
In this project, I worked on analyzing CRM sales data to understand how leads move through different stages of the pipeline. The main goal was to identify where drop-offs happen, which sources perform better, and how revenue is distributed across stages.

---

## 🌍 Real-World Relevance  
This project is inspired by how real CRM tools like Salesforce are used in companies. It reflects practical scenarios where businesses track leads, monitor opportunities, and use data to improve sales performance and decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  
- Python  
- Pandas  
- Matplotlib  

---

## 📊 What I Did  
- Cleaned raw CRM data by handling missing values and inconsistencies  
- Analyzed lead conversion across different pipeline stages  
- Studied revenue contribution at each stage  
- Identified where most leads drop off  
- Built a dashboard to visualize overall sales performance  

---

## 📈 Key Metrics  
- Lead Conversion Rate  
- Revenue by Opportunity Stage  
- Win Rate by Lead Source  
- Sales Funnel Drop-off  

---

## 💡 Key Insights  
- A significant number of leads drop at the negotiation stage  
- Referral leads perform much better compared to other sources  
- Only a few lead sources contribute to most of the revenue  

---

## 🚀 Business Impact  
This analysis can help businesses:
- Focus more on high-converting lead sources  
- Improve weak stages in the sales process  
- Make better decisions on marketing and sales strategies
